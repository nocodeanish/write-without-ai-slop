Title: Spoke count shows 0 after saving a repair job on the Example Cycle Works workshop tablet

Steps:
1. Open the workshop tablet app (v2.4.1, Android 13) and tap New Repair.
2. Choose wheel truing and enter 36 in Spoke count.
3. Tap Save.

Expected: the job list shows 36 in the Spoke count column.
Actual: the job list shows 0. Reopening the job shows 36 again, so the value is stored but not displayed in the list.

Happens every time on the two tablets in the shop. It does not happen in the browser version. Screenshot attached: job-list-0.png.
