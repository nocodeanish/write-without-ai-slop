Title: Let members renew a loan online

Members can only renew at the desk. They should be able to renew from the Loans page.

Scenarios

**Given** a loan is due in 3 days and no one has reserved the book
**When** the member presses "Renew"
**Then** the due date moves 14 days later and a confirmation shows.

**Given** a loan is due in 3 days and another member has reserved the book
**When** the member presses "Renew"
**Then** the renewal is refused and the message says the book is reserved.

**Given** a loan is already overdue
**When** the member opens the Loans page
**Then** the "Renew" button is disabled.

Out of scope: renewing by email and changing the loan length.
